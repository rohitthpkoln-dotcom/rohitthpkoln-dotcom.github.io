Rohit Mukherjee

I am an Alexander von Humboldt postdoctoral fellow at the Institute for Theoretical Physics, University of Cologne, where I work in Simon Trebst's group on computational condensed matter physics.

I completed my PhD at IIT Kanpur under the supervision of Arijit Kundu and Avinash Singh, and during 2023–2025 I was a Fulbright fellow at Cornell in Debanjan Chowdhury's group.

My research lies at the intersection of theoretical and numerical approaches to emergent phenomena in quantum many-body systems. I am particularly interested in thermalization, chaos, measurement-induced phase transitions, quantum error correction, and machine learning.

I have also been a visiting student at ICTP in Trieste and at UC Riverside in Ran Cheng's group, and I serve as an active referee and member of the American Physical Society.
