Research Interests

My research lies at the intersection of theoretical and numerical approaches to emergent phenomena in quantum many-body systems. I am particularly interested in thermalization, chaos, measurement-induced phase transitions, quantum error correction, and machine learning.

I use tools including exact diagonalization and tensor network techniques. In earlier work I studied topological transport in quantum magnets and spin liquids, the role of quantum geometry in transport phenomena, and exotic states in Weyl semimetals and topological insulators.

Across these settings, my work has centred on developing theoretical and numerical tools to understand nonequilibrium quantum dynamics, quantum information, and transport in strongly correlated systems.
